This application creates and displays 2 moveable player characters, Mario and Luigi. Mario is controlled by both the arrow keys and WASD keys. He can move
in all 4 directions. Luigi is controlled by the left and right mouse buttons. This means that he can only move 2 directions, left and right. Both characters
are able to run. Mario runs when the space bar is pressed; Luigi runs when the scroll wheel is pressed down. The application can be closed by pressing the
escape key.
